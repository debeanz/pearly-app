Compatibility

What runs on Pearly

Every report is sent from inside the app by someone playing the game, with the build, device and settings they used. A game's rating is the one most of its reports agree on.

Perfect

Plays like it does on a PC.

Playable

Small problems that don't get in the way.

Runs

Reaches gameplay, with problems you notice.

Boots

Starts, but can't really be played.

Broken

Doesn't start, or crashes right away.

Played something? Report it.

In Pearly, open a game's ⋯ menu, on the Games tab or from the toolbar while you play, and choose Report Compatibility. The game's name, your device and the build fill themselves in; you pick a rating and say what happened. If a game crashes, Pearly offers to report it right then.
